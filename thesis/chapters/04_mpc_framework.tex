%     and smooth approximations.

%   * Continuous current stimulation.
%   * Pulse-based stimulation: Mixed-Integer formulation (binary scheduling variables delta_{j|k}), continuous relaxation
%     with schedule recovery, and continuous pulse-timing alternatives.
% - Numerical Transcription and Real-Time Solvers:
%   * Direct single shooting (sensitivities across rollouts) vs. multiple shooting.
%   * Resulting optimization classes: Convex QP (linear DMDc + quadratic cost) vs. non-convex NLP/SQP (nonlinear MLP).
%   * Computational tractability and update deadline considerations.
