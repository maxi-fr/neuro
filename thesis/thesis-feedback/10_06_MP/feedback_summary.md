## 2. Top-Down Structure & Narrative Flow (12 comments)

> [!NOTE]
> **Author Clarification on TVB and "The Solver" (STR-02, STR-05, STR-08, STR-09):**
> TVB (\ac{tvb}) is **only** used to supply the default 76-region structural connectome matrices (structural connectivity weights $\mathbf{W}$ and tract lengths / conduction delays), **not** for numerical simulation or solving.
> The reviewer feedback attributing solving to TVB (STR-02, STR-09) was misguided because the draft text repeatedly used the phrase "the solver". This terminology reads like code / software documentation and should generally be avoided in academic thesis writing.
> **Action principle:** Eliminate "the solver" entirely. Delineate between the theoretical continuous-time delay differential system and its discrete-time numerical simulation (stochastic Heun integration, delay quantization with step size $\Delta t_\mathrm{p}$). Introduce TVB strictly as an anatomical data source.
